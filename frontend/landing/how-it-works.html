Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take? +
The entire process takes 8-12 seconds on average. This includes camera permission, face calibration, challenge completion, and verification. It's faster than typing a CAPTCHA.
What if the user doesn't have a camera? +
You can configure a fallback method (email verification, SMS code, or traditional CAPTCHA) for users without cameras. Our API returns a "no_camera" status for these cases.
Can bots fake the motion challenges? +
No. We analyze real-time facial geometry and movement physics. Deepfakes, pre-recorded videos, and static images all fail our integrity checks. The randomness makes it computationally impossible to pre-compute responses.
Is video data stored? +
No. Videos are analyzed in real-time and immediately deleted. We only store metadata (trust score, timestamp, device ID) for fraud detection. Zero personally identifiable images are retained.
Does it work on mobile? +
Yes! Certivo works on iOS Safari, Android Chrome, and all modern mobile browsers. The experience is optimized for both front and rear cameras.
