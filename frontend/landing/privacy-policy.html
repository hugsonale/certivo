Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 20, 2026

At Certivo, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our motion-based human verification service.

🛡️ Your Rights ⏱️ Data Retention ✉️ Contact Us 🖨️ Print Policy

1 Introduction

Certivo, Inc. ("Certivo," "we," "us," or "our") provides motion-based human verification services to help businesses prevent bot attacks and fraudulent activity. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through our website, API, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

💡 Key Principle
Privacy by Design: Certivo is built with privacy at its core. We analyze video data in real-time and do not store video recordings. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our verification services and prevent fraud.

2 Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you interact with our Services.

2.1 Information from End Users (Verification Participants)

When an end user completes a verification challenge through our Service, we collect:

Data Type What We Collect Purpose
Video Analysis Data Facial landmark coordinates, movement patterns, liveness scores Verify human presence and prevent bot attacks
Device Information Device ID, browser type, operating system, screen resolution Fraud detection and device trust scoring
Technical Metadata IP address, timestamp, session ID, challenge type Security, analytics, and service improvement
Custom Metadata Any metadata provided by our business customers Customer's business purposes (as data processor)
🎥 Important: Video Recording Policy
We do NOT store video recordings. All video data is analyzed in real-time using computer vision algorithms and immediately deleted after analysis is complete. We retain only the extracted metadata (facial landmarks, movement scores) necessary for fraud detection and service improvement.

2.2 Information from Business Customers

When you sign up for a Certivo account as a business customer, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, company name, billing address
  • Payment Information: Credit card details (processed securely by our payment processor, Stripe)
  • API Usage Data: API keys, request logs, verification metrics, error rates
  • Communications: Support tickets, emails, feedback, and other communications with us

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website or use our Services, we automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, features used, click patterns
  • Device Data: Browser type, device type, operating system, language preferences
  • Log Data: IP address, referral URLs, access times, error logs
  • Cookies: See Section 8 for details on our cookie usage

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

3.1 To Provide Our Services

  • Process verification requests and return trust scores to our business customers
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity, bot attacks, and abuse
  • Generate device fingerprints and trust levels for fraud prevention
  • Maintain and improve the accuracy of our verification algorithms

3.2 To Improve Our Services

  • Analyze usage patterns to identify bugs and improve performance
  • Train and refine our machine learning models for better accuracy
  • Conduct research and development on new verification methods
  • Generate anonymized analytics and insights about verification trends

3.3 To Communicate With You

  • Send account notifications, API updates, and service announcements
  • Respond to customer support inquiries and technical issues
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent, and you can opt out anytime)
  • Conduct surveys and gather feedback about our Services

3.4 For Legal and Security Purposes

  • Comply with legal obligations, court orders, and regulatory requirements
  • Protect our rights, property, and safety, and that of our users
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, security breaches, and illegal activities
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements
⚖️ Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)
For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, we process your data based on:

• Contractual Necessity: To provide our verification services
• Legitimate Interests: Fraud prevention, security, and service improvement
• Consent: Marketing communications and optional features
• Legal Obligations: Compliance with applicable laws

4 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 With Our Business Customers

When you complete a verification challenge requested by one of our business customers (e.g., during an airdrop signup or account creation), we share the verification results with that customer, including:

  • Trust score and verification status (passed/failed)
  • Device trust level and fraud risk indicators
  • Session ID and timestamp
  • Any custom metadata the customer provided

Note: In this scenario, we act as a data processor on behalf of our business customer (the data controller). The customer's privacy policy governs their use of your information.

4.2 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our Services:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (AWS) for hosting and data storage
  • Payment Processing: Stripe for secure payment processing
  • Email Services: SendGrid for transactional and marketing emails
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for website usage analytics (anonymized)
  • Customer Support: Zendesk for support ticket management

All service providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

4.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  • Protect and defend our rights or property
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing or fraud
  • Protect the safety of our users or the public

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you of any such change and your choices regarding your information.

⚠️ Data Controller vs. Data Processor
When you use certivo.com: Certivo is the data controller of your information.

When you complete verification for a business customer: That business is the data controller, and Certivo is the data processor. The business's privacy policy governs their use of your data.

5 Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Video Recordings Immediately deleted Real-time analysis only; never stored
Verification Metadata 90 days Fraud detection and analytics
Device Fingerprints 1 year Trust scoring and fraud prevention
API Logs 30 days Debugging and security monitoring
Account Information Duration of account + 7 years Tax and legal compliance
Anonymized Analytics Indefinitely Cannot be linked to individuals

5.1 Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time (see Section 6 for details). After deletion:

  • Your data will be permanently removed from our active systems within 30 days
  • Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days before automatic deletion
  • Anonymized data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely
  • Some data may be retained if required by law or necessary for legal claims

6 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

6.1 Rights Under GDPR (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent where we rely on it
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

6.2 Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the "sale" of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@certivo.com
  • Mail: Certivo, Inc., Attn: Privacy Officer, [Address]
  • Online Form: Privacy Request Form

We will respond to your request within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

✅ No Discrimination Promise
We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights. You will not receive different pricing, service levels, or product quality for making a privacy request.
