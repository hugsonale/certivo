# Data sheet: synthetic-v0

## Why it exists
To give the ML workspace something to run on (step P0-30), so data versioning, experiment tracking, model cards and reproduction from a clean checkout can be tested before any real data has been approved (step P0-31).

## What is in it
2,400 grey pictures of 16 by 16 pixels, each with a label (1 if a faint square stamp is present in the middle, 0 if not), a made-up "device" group (0, 1 or 2) and a split (train, validation or test). Half the pictures carry the stamp. The noise grows with the group number.
It contains **no people, no faces, no video and no audio**.

## Where it came from
Generated by `ml/certivo_ml/synthetic.py` from the settings in `ml/params.yaml` (seed 20261105). Anyone can regenerate it and must get the same bytes.

## Licence and permission
Generated by this repository. No third-party rights. No consent is needed because no person is involved.

## Fingerprint
`data_sha256` in `ml/metrics/baseline-synthetic-v0.json`. The data itself lives in `ml/data/synthetic-v0/` and is **not committed to git**; `dvc repro` rebuilds it.

## Known problems
It is artificial and easy. It says nothing about real faces, real cameras, real compression or real attacks, and no result on it may be quoted as a measure of Certivo's accuracy.

## Who may use it, and for how long it is kept
Anyone on the project. It is rebuilt on demand and never needs to be kept.
