# Data sheet: {{dataset name}}

One data sheet per dataset, filled in BEFORE the data is used for training (guide step P0-31). A dataset without a finished data sheet is not used.

## Why it exists
What is it for, and who asked for it?

## What is in it
Number of items, what each item is, labels and how they were made, any groups or attributes recorded, and what is NOT in it.

## Where it came from
Who made or collected it, when, and how. For people's images or video: how each person agreed, and what they agreed to. **No scraping of people's faces.**

## Licence and permission
The licence, in the owner's words, and whether it allows commercial use. If it does not, say so plainly: the dataset is then research-only and may not train a shipped model.

## Fingerprint
The `data_sha256` printed by the pipeline, and where the data is stored (never in git).

## Known problems
Gaps, biases, labelling errors, and anything that makes it unrepresentative.

## Who may use it, and for how long it is kept
Access, retention and deletion.
