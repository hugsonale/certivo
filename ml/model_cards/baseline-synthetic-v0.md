# Model card: baseline-synthetic-v0

> **This model was trained and tested on SYNTHETIC data generated by this repository. It exists to test the ML workspace. It says nothing about how well any real model would work on real people, and no accuracy claim may be made from it.**

## Model details

- **Name and version:** baseline-synthetic-v0
- **Kind:** Nearest-class-average classifier with a calibrated temperature (plain NumPy, no learned layers).
- **Input and output:** Input: a 16x16 grey picture. Output: a score and a probability that the picture carries the faint central stamp.
- **How it was trained:** `dvc repro` in `ml/`: generate the data, train on the training split, pick the temperature on the validation split, evaluate once on the test split.
- **Code and settings:** this repository's `ml/` folder; every setting is in `ml/params.yaml`.

## Intended use

To prove that experiments in this repository can be tracked, versioned and reproduced from a clean checkout, and as the first line in the comparison table of later models.

## Not for

Anything to do with real faces, real calls, liveness or deepfake detection. It has never seen a person and must not be described as detecting anything.

## Training data

- **Dataset:** synthetic-v0 (data sheet: `ml/datasheets/synthetic-v0.md`)
- **Fingerprint:** `edce86f069657bd9163dc10666cba6dd07f8af720a32b04e16dc0515ff16a024`
- **Origin and permission:** Generated by `ml/certivo_ml/synthetic.py` from a fixed seed. No person, no face, no scraped or licensed data.
- **Split:** 1440 training, 480 validation, 480 test.

## Limits and risks

The task is easy and artificial. The groups are invented, and the third was given more noise on purpose, so differences between groups here show that the reporting works, not that any real group is treated worse.

## Fairness and robustness

Not evaluated. A fairness and robustness evaluation on real subgroups, devices and compression levels is step P0-39 and needs real data.

## Promotion status

**Not promoted.** No external claim may be made about this model. Promotion needs the gate of step P0-40 (detection latency and false-trigger rate measured and recorded), which a synthetic model cannot pass.

## Reproduce

```
cd ml
pip install -r requirements.txt -c constraints.txt
dvc repro
```
A clean checkout must give this card and the metrics file unchanged.
